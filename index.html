Specialist technical programs

IECEx

Explosive atmospheres, hazardous area classification and certified equipment use, for safe installation, inspection and maintenance that meets international standards and reduces fire and explosion risk.

  • Explosive atmosphere awareness
  • Hazardous area classification
  • Certified equipment handling
  • Inspection and maintenance
  • IECEx standards compliance

Thermal Insulation

Skills to select, install and maintain insulation systems safely, improving energy efficiency, reducing heat loss and protecting equipment across industrial and construction sites.

  • Insulation material knowledge
  • Proper installation techniques
  • Energy efficiency improvement
  • Safety compliance standards
  • Maintenance and inspection

BGAS CSWIP

Welding inspection, non-destructive testing awareness and quality control, ensuring compliance with international standards and the integrity of welded structures.

  • Welding inspection principles
  • NDT awareness basics
  • Quality control procedures
  • Standards compliance knowledge
  • Inspection reporting skills

High Angle Rope Rescue

Practical skills to access, secure and evacuate casualties from elevated or steep environments, with proper rope systems, anchor setup and teamwork.
